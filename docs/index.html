Stocks at year-end

The main series are BIS total assets at year-end, adjusted for statistical breaks. Euro area means the consolidated Eurosystem. Dollar changes also reflect exchange rates and asset valuations; GDP shares also reflect nominal GDP.

Reserves & exchange rates

World Bank / IMF international reserves are external assets controlled by monetary authorities. Their scope and valuation differ from balance-sheet accounts, so they are a related indicator, not a harmonized component breakdown. Monetary gold is the difference between reserves including and excluding gold.

The implied FX rate is adjusted local-currency assets divided by dollar assets. In the FX index, an increase means depreciation against the US dollar. The index compares movements; raw rates have different currency units.

Coverage & sources

Missing reserve values stay missing; lines do not connect across gaps. No incomplete 2026 observation is treated as a full year. Russia is outside this BIS sample. Hong Kong SAR uses the Exchange Fund; Norges Bank excludes the Government Pension Fund Global.
